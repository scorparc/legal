Principle: your data stays on your device

LevelCamp stores vehicle profiles with dimensions, phone positions, calibrations, leveling aids, checklists, settings and the last confirmed Pro status exclusively in the app's private storage on your device. Motion sensors are evaluated only in memory to calculate inclination; raw sensor data is neither stored nor transmitted. There is no user account, no own server, no advertising and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. Location, camera, microphone and contacts are not used.

Local remote display

Only when you start a remote display session does LevelCamp use network service discovery (NSD/mDNS) to find a second device running LevelCamp on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot and connect to it directly via TCP. Only a temporary pairing code, a session key and the current instruction, stability, level state, a sequence number and a timestamp are transmitted. The credentials are valid for at most 15 minutes and exist only while the app is running. There is no internet server for this feature; the data does not leave your local network.

Backup and export

Android backup is disabled for LevelCamp. A backup file with your vehicles, leveling aids, checklists, calibrations and settings is created only when you start the export yourself, and it is saved at the location you choose in the Android file dialog. The diagnostic export contains the app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, available sensors, the number of your entries and your settings, but no dimensions or names. You decide what happens to exported files.

Purchases via Google Play

The one-time Pro unlock is processed through Google Play Billing. Google processes purchase and payment data under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase status, stores the unlock locally and receives no payment data. The legal basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Legal texts and external links

At your request, the app opens the legal notice and privacy policy via GitHub Pages (scorparc.github.io) in your browser. The app itself does not fetch any data from the internet for this. Processing in the browser is governed by the information of the browser and of GitHub.

Deletion

You can delete vehicles, leveling aids and checklists in the app. You remove all local data via Android → Apps → LevelCamp → Clear storage or by uninstalling. As there is no account and no server, the provider holds no data that would need to be deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have, in particular, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As app data is stored locally under your control, you can inspect, change or delete it there. Google is the responsible contact for purchase data processed by Google.
