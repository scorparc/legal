Local game data

Arrowtide stores progress, level stars, records, daily challenges, streaks, coins, boosters, themes, purchase status and settings in a DataStore file on your device. There is no user account, own server, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. Camera, microphone, contacts and precise location are not used. Sharing opens the Android share sheet only when you choose it, with a result image created locally.

Ads and consent

Google AdMob shows occasional interstitial ads between games and optional rewarded videos for continuing, boosters or extra coins. There are no banners. Before ad requests, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether consent is required and shows a form if necessary. The app requests ads only when UMP permits this. Google may process IP address (and approximate location inferred from it), advertising and app set IDs, app interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Privacy options appear in Settings when UMP requires them. You can reset or delete the ad ID in Android. The legal basis is consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), or legitimate interest in funding the app for permissible non-personalised ads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Purchases and Play services

The one-time Remove Ads purchase uses Google Play Billing; it removes interstitial ads, while optional rewarded videos remain available. Google processes purchase and payment data under its own terms. The app receives the purchase status and stores the entitlement locally; it receives no payment details. After suitable results, Google Play In-App Review may be opened. No game progress is sent to Google for this.

Android backup

Android backup and device transfer are limited to the DataStore progress file. Whether a cloud backup occurs depends on your Android and Google account settings. The app does not back up ad SDK data.

Deletion

Delete local data through Android → Apps → Arrowtide → Clear storage or by uninstalling. Manage any Android cloud backup in your Google account. Since there is no app account or own server, there is no separate deletion request for server-side game progress.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have, in particular, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. App data is generally stored locally under your control, so you can inspect, change or delete it there. For data Google processes for advertising or backups, Google is the relevant contact.
