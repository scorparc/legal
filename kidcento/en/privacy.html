Privacy policy

1. App and controller

This policy describes the Android app Kidcento, a virtual allowance ledger for parents. The provider and contact for privacy requests is Marc Neumann, Schwabenstraße 47, 71101 Schönaich, Germany. Email: vardholt@gmail.com. The app does not hold or transfer real money.

2. Data on your device

The app stores the child names and avatar colours you enter, allowance schedules, amounts, currency, transaction types, categories, descriptions, transaction and schedule dates, and savings goals. It also stores preferences such as language, appearance and onboarding status. These records are kept in the local app database or app storage. There is no user account, provider-operated app server, automatic cloud synchronisation, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service. We do not receive child names or ledger records through the app. The app is intended for adults; children do not need an account.

3. Premium and Google Play

Optional Premium access is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. On startup, purchase or restoration, the app may contact Play to request product information and purchase entitlements and acknowledge purchases. Google processes purchase, account, device and connection data under its own terms. The app does not send your child profiles or ledger records to Google for these requests. We do not operate a payment or receipt-verification server and do not receive card details through the app. A signed purchase receipt and its signature are stored locally to retain verified Premium access offline. The receipt may include the product ID, package name, purchase time, order ID and purchase token. Refunds and revocations are recognised on the next successful Play query; the offline receipt has no fixed expiry. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Play purchase information: https://support.google.com/googleplay/.

4. Export and restoration

You can save or select unencrypted JSON backups and, with Premium, CSV files using the Android file picker. A JSON backup contains local child, schedule, transaction, goal and preference data, including archived children and locally retained deleted transactions; it does not contain your Premium purchase receipt. CSV exports contain ledger data. Depending on your selected document provider, a file may be stored on your device or in cloud storage. The app does not operate a cloud service or integrate a Google Drive API. A storage provider you select processes files under its own terms. Protect these files from unauthorised access because they can contain personal data. Restoring a backup replaces the current local ledger data. Premium entitlement is restored separately through Google Play.

5. Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you remove it. Archiving preserves records and pauses the schedule. Deleting an individual transaction retains the record locally with a deletion flag, preventing automatic allowance credits from being recreated; it is hidden from the history and balance but may still be included in JSON backups. Permanently deleting a child removes that child's schedules, transactions and goals from the local database. Android's app settings let you clear all app data; uninstalling also removes local app storage, including the receipt cache. Ordinary deletion does not guarantee forensic erasure of storage. Exported files remain independent copies and must be deleted separately at their storage location. Android automatic app backup and app-controlled device transfer are disabled; make your own backups of important records.

6. Legal bases and support

Local data you manage yourself is processed technically to provide the app functions you request; the provider has no remote access to it. Where we process personal data in support requests, we do so to handle contract-related enquiries under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR or based on our legitimate interest in answering other enquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Send only the information needed for your request and avoid real child data or full backups. Support messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet any statutory retention duties. Google's information and legal bases apply to its independent processing and any international transfers.

Where the legal requirements apply and we process data about you, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You may withdraw any consent with effect for the future. Contact vardholt@gmail.com. We cannot access records stored solely on your device; use the editing, export and deletion features or Android's app settings for those records.
